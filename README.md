# 🛍️ MUC01 Retail Category Intelligence

### 📊 Retail Product Performance & Business Intelligence using Python

> **Calibo AI Academy – Phase 1 Mini Use Case 01**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**MUC01 Retail Category Intelligence** is a data analysis project that studies retail sales transactions to understand **category performance, product contribution, discount patterns, and sales trends**.

The analysis helps a **Category Manager** understand which categories and products are performing well, which are declining, and where business attention may be required.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to identify:

- 📈 Growing and declining product categories
- 🏆 Top and bottom performing products
- 💰 Revenue performance
- 🏷️ Discount effectiveness
- 📅 Day-of-week sales patterns
- 📆 Monthly revenue trends
- 💡 Actionable business insights

---

## 🧠 CBIM Problem Canvas

### Situation

A regional retail chain operates multiple stores and sells products across different categories. The Category Manager needs data-driven insights for supplier review and category planning.

### Complication

It is difficult to identify which categories and products are performing well and which are declining by looking at raw transaction data.

### Business Question

> **Which categories are growing or declining, which products are driving performance, and what should the Category Manager prioritize or deprioritize?**

### Success Definition

The analysis should provide clear charts, tables, and business insights that support category management and supplier discussions.
